The Chain of Torah

Same story as the timeline on the wall — just the thread that matters most: how what was given at Sinai turned into all the books we still study today. Two tracks run the whole way down. Tap any link to open it.

Two Torahs, one Sinai

Tradition holds that two things were given at Sinai, not one: the written text, and alongside it the explanation of how to actually carry it out. Everything below descends from one or the other, and knowing which is which makes the whole chain far easier to read.

Torah SheBichtav — the Written Torah The text itself: the Five Books, then the Prophets and Writings. It was written from the start, and then it closed. Nothing has been added to it in roughly 2,400 years.
Torah SheBe'al Peh — the Oral Torah How that text is actually lived: what "don't work on Shabbat" means in practice. For centuries this was memory only, never written down — the dashed rail below. It is still growing today.
Sinai
Both tracks leave Sinai together. The written one closes early. The oral one runs on unwritten for centuries — the dashed rail — until the fear of losing it forces it into a book at stop 3.
